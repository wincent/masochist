I started using [Resilio Sync](https://www.resilio.com/) in late 2023 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resilio_Sync)). Design is very similar to [Syncthing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syncthing), but has an iOS client, and it's the latter that determined the choice when I was looking at alternatives to [Sync](/wiki/Sync).

**Update (2026):** Syncthing now has a pretty sweet third-party iOS app in the form of [Synctrain](https://github.com/pixelspark/sushitrain), and [Resilio got acquired](https://www.nasuni.com/nasuni-acquires-resilio/) and the new owner hasn't shown much interest in continuing development, so the calculus has changed and I wouldn't recommend Resilio Sync right now.
