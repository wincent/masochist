title: Standing desk
---

Three positions:

1. Seated: 68cm
2. Standing: 109cm
3. "Parked": 90cm[^parked]

[^parked]: High enough to shove the chair under the desk, when I'm not using the desk.

Height limits:

- Upper height limit: 110cm[^higher]
- Lower height limit: 65cm[^lower]

[^higher]: Desk could go higher, but cables plugged into the back of the computer aren't long enough.

[^lower]: Desk could go lower, but computer under the desk is in the way.

Resetting the desk:

1. Press and hold "⌄". When desk reaches the minimum height (65cm), continue holding the button for 5 seconds until you hear a beep.
2. If necessary, restore remembered positions by setting height, pressing "M", then pressing the desired number.
3. Set lower and upper limits by moving the desk to the corresponding positions and pressing and holding "⌄" and "⌃" at the same time for 5 seconds until you hear a beep[^limits].

[^limits]: It's important that you set the limits because the going too low will cause the desk to collide with the computer under the desk, and going to high will pull and/or yank the cables when they reach their maximum extension.
